Ḥaasi al-Layl — Privacy Policy

Effective 2 October 2026 · App: Ḥaasi al-Layl (Android) · Developer: Ahsanul Houqe

Ḥaasi al-Layl shows prayer times, the Qibla direction, the weather, supplications and the 99 Names of Allah. It has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. The developer does not run a server for the app and does not collect, store, sell or share your personal data. This page explains the little data the app uses and which services it reaches.

Location

With your permission, the app reads your device's location (precise or approximate, whichever you allow) to calculate prayer times, the Qibla direction and the weather for where you are. Location is used only while the app is open; the app does not request background location. The last location is kept on your phone only, so prayer times keep working offline.

Services the app contacts

The prayer times and Qibla are calculated on your phone and need no internet. When you are online, the app sends the minimum needed to these services:

ServiceWhat is sentWhy
BigDataCloudCoordinates To turn coordinates into a place name, when the phone's own geocoder cannot
Open-MeteoCoordinates of the selected place; city names you type in search Weather forecast; finding a city by name
ipinfo.ioYour IP address (sent with any internet request) Only when the device cannot give a location: to estimate your city, shown as "approximate"
Google Play services (on your phone)— Android's own location and geocoding services may be used to read your position and name it

These services receive the request as any web service does, including your IP address, and handle it under their own privacy policies. The app sends no name, email, contacts, account or device identifier.

Notifications and alarms

Prayer, forbidden-time and Tahajjud alerts are scheduled on your phone. Nothing about them leaves the device.

Data on your phone

Your settings, chosen city, prayer log, Tasbih counts and bookmarks are stored on your phone only. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes them.

Feedback email

If you tap "Send feedback", your own email app opens a message to the developer. Anything you choose to send is used only to answer you and improve the app.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
