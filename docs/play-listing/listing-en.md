## Full description (max 4000)

```
Ḥaasi al-Layl is a quiet companion for the day and the night: accurate prayer times worked out on your phone, an alarm that rings on the minute, and the remembrance of Allah close at hand. No account, no ads, no tracking.

PRAYER TIMES THAT WORK OFFLINE
• Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha calculated on the phone for exactly where you are
• The current prayer, when it ends and how long is left; the next prayer with a countdown
• Calculation method chosen for your country (Karachi for Bangladesh, India and Pakistan; Umm al-Qura for the Gulf; and others), changeable in Settings
• Madhab for Asr: Shafi'i, Maliki, Hanbali or Hanafi
• Mark each prayer as prayed

PRAYER ALARM
• Rings at each prayer time, over the lock screen like an alarm clock
• A gentle reminder five minutes later, skipped if you have already prayed
• Optional Tahajjud reminder when the last third of the night begins
• Optional alerts for the forbidden times for voluntary prayer, with their hadith references
• A reliability check in Settings that shows what your phone needs for alarms to ring on time

RAMADAN
• Sehri and Iftar times, with the days on which fasting is recommended, discouraged or forbidden
• Hijri dates, with the month start following Saudi Arabia and the moon

QIBLA
• Compass bearing to the Kaaba from where you stand, with the distance
• Magnetic declination corrected automatically

TASBIH
• SubhanAllah, Alhamdulillah, Allahu Akbar, Astaghfirullah, La ilaha illallah, Salawat, the Dua of Yunus and more
• Vibration on each count and at the end of each round; counts are saved

HISN AL-MUSLIM
• The complete Fortress of the Muslim: 133 chapters, 302 supplications
• Arabic, pronunciation, translation, how many times to say it, and the source
• Search in any language, and bookmarks

THE 99 NAMES OF ALLAH
• Each name in Arabic with its meaning and pronunciation
• Tap a name for a short explanation and a verse of the Qur'an where it occurs

MORE
• Hijri, Gregorian and Bangla calendars
• The sun and moon across the sky, with the moon's phase
• A daily ayah and hadith
• Languages: English, العربية, বাংলা, Español, Português

PRIVACY
Your location is used only to calculate prayer times and the Qibla. It never leaves the phone except to look up the name of your town. Nothing is stored on any server.

A note on translations: the translations of Hisn al-Muslim and the explanations of the 99 Names in this app are our own and have not been reviewed by a scholar. Please check with a trusted source, and write to us if you find a mistake.

User guide: https://www.ahsanulhouqe.com/haasi-al-layl/guide/
Feedback: contact@ahsanulhouqe.com
```
